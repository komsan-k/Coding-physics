# Course Assessment and Requirements

## Assessment Scheme

| Component                          |   Weight |
| ---------------------------------- | -------: |
| Completion of 10 Course Modules    |      80% |
| Class Discussion and Participation |      10% |
| Weekly Progress Reports            |      10% |
| **Total**                          | **100%** |

---

## Course Details

### 1. Course Enrollment

Students are required to enroll in the following Coursera course:

**General Physics**

https://www.coursera.org/programs/artificial-intelligence-and-system-eng-65etz/learn/general-physics-odc#modules

---

### 2. Completion of Course Modules (80%)

Students must complete all **10 modules** of the General Physics course within **5 weeks**.

* Complete approximately **2 modules per week**.
* Submit a **weekly progress report** documenting completed modules and learning outcomes.
* The weekly progress report contributes **10%** of the final grade.
* The completion of the 10 modules contributes **80%** of the final grade.

**Deadline:** 3 July 2026

#### Late Submission Policy

Modules completed after the deadline will incur a penalty of **2% per day** deducted from the module completion score.

---

### 3. Class Discussion (10%)

Students are expected to actively participate in class discussions and share insights gained from the course materials.

Class discussion and participation contribute **10%** of the final grade.

*Additional laboratory activities may be assigned when necessary or under exceptional circumstances.*

## Important Notes

1. Students are responsible for monitoring their progress and ensuring timely completion of all modules.
2. Weekly progress reports must be submitted according to the schedule announced by the instructor.
